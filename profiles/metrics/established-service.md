# Metric profile: Established service

This profile suggests common health signals for an established service/product. Select only what reflects real project outcomes and commitments.

Possible useful signals include:

- user/task success for important workflows;
- availability and error rate where service continuity matters;
- latency or responsiveness for consequential paths;
- recovery time and deployment/change failure behavior;
- accessibility of primary workflows where people interact with the system;
- support/incident burden and recurring failure classes;
- compatibility/regression behavior where external users or systems depend on stable interfaces;
- operational observability sufficient to diagnose important failures.

Thresholds should come from project needs or evidence. If an important outcome is not measured, preserve that as a measurement gap rather than inventing a target.
