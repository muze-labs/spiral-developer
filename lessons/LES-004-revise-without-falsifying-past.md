# LES-004 — Preserve the ability to change our minds

**Status:** accepted as process principle  
**Source:** human discussion, 2026-08-11

## Observation

Requirements, interpretations, designs, implementation causes, engineering preferences, and even process rules can all become obsolete as evidence accumulates.

## Lesson

Version beliefs and preferences so later revisions do not rewrite why older decisions were made. The system should be able to say that a historical decision made sense under an earlier understanding or culture while acknowledging that the current project would choose differently.

## Scope

Cross-cutting process principle. Existing Git-versioned artifact semantics already provide much of the mechanism; lessons and explicit culture extend it to the production environment itself.
