# Lesson: Keep warning lenses separate from culture and core

## Observation / evidence

Some recurring concerns are neither trust invariants nor engineering preferences. They are patterns worth noticing: premature closure, authority drift, evidence overreach, problematic composition, loss of optionality, meaningful exclusion, and model closure.

Treating them as culture would make ethical/risk scrutiny look like an aesthetic preference. Treating them as Spiral core would make one evolving set of concerns universal and difficult to replace.

## Lesson

Warning heuristics should be explicit, versioned, replaceable project inputs of their own. Projects should adopt the lenses they want active, and agents should surface them proportionately rather than turning them into automatic prohibitions.

## Scope

Spiral-process candidate. This is about how the methodology represents a reusable class of project guidance.

## Confidence / limits

High confidence in the separation. Lower confidence in the completeness and future organization of the initial warning set. Profiles may split, merge, or move to independent repositories.

## Proposed consequence

Introduce `WarningProfile` and explicit project adoption, document significance/disposition semantics, and provide a starter profile without making it mandatory.

## Outcome after adoption

To be evaluated through dogfooding. Watch especially for warning fatigue, moralizing prose, automatic blocking, and failure to surface a genuinely consequential concern.
